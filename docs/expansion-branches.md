# Supply district and Simulation facility

Both leads open from **Home → Quest board** after taking the case. Choose **Open lead graph** to inspect the sequence and reference material, or **Pursue lead** to resume the next unfinished encounter. They can be played in either order and do not depend on the other clues.

## Supply district

1. Dispatch Drone — greedy choices and the signed shipment order.
2. Reserve Guard — planning across future dispatches.
3. Forecast Auditor — inventory decisions under demand uncertainty.
4. Quartermaster — mixed questions on planning, inventory and probabilistic forecasts; recovers the battery handoff window.

## Simulation facility

1. Training Dummy — record a baseline policy.
2. Reward Seeker — account for future value instead of only immediate reward.
3. Checkpoint Echo — evaluate a learned policy under changed conditions.
4. Warden — mixed questions on reinforcement learning and model evaluation; recovers ECHO-7's escape policy and a safe containment condition.

Each encounter uses questions linked to the relevant supplied chapter and has its own readable tell, ACT choice and retry. The final clue in each branch unlocks only after the preceding encounters. Existing saves keep their completed progress; a case already closed before save format 5 stays closed, while an unfinished case needs both new clues before ECHO-7 becomes available.
