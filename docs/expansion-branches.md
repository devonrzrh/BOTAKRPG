# Supply district and Simulation facility

Both leads open from **Home → Quest board** after taking the case. Choose **Open lead graph** to inspect the sequence and reference material, or **Pursue lead** to resume the next unfinished activity. They can be played in either order and do not depend on the other clues.

## Supply district

1. Dispatch Drone — greedy choices and the signed shipment order.
2. Reserve Guard — planning across future dispatches.
3. The reserve schedule — sort four battery assignments by urgency while preserving stock for later demand.
4. Forecast Auditor — inventory decisions under demand uncertainty.
5. The handoff ledger — search timestamped records from the order desk, charging bays, relay reader and depot ledger to locate the 02:14 transfer.
6. Quartermaster — mixed questions on planning, inventory and probabilistic forecasts; recovers the battery handoff window.

## Simulation facility

1. Training Dummy — record a baseline policy.
2. Reward Seeker — account for future value instead of only immediate reward.
3. Checkpoint Echo — evaluate a learned policy under changed conditions.
4. Test the learned route — find a safe path after Relay A goes offline and compare it with the training route.
5. Warden — mixed questions on reinforcement learning and model evaluation; recovers ECHO-7's escape policy and a safe containment condition.

After the five branch clues are filed, **Reconstruct the evidence** opens a sorting board that combines the signed audit, receipt, handshake, handoff forecast and containment condition into one verified route. ECHO-7 remains locked until that sequence is complete. The final choice to spare or defeat ECHO-7 is saved in the journal and recorded in the clue ledger.

Each combat encounter uses questions linked to the relevant supplied chapter and has its own readable tell, ACT choice and retry. Journal migration marks newly added activities complete when an older save already cleared the later encounter, without paying their rewards again.
